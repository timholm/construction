## Full description text (the video's narration, verbatim)

The full map is free. Link in bio. This is one building company, drawn as a map. At the top: what the company already runs on. The office inbox, the phone line, plans, the job schedule, estimates, trade texts, contracts and the accounts. Around the middle, seven departments. Leads (9 AI agents) answers every enquiry the same day, asks about the site, the scope and the budget, and books the site visit. Estimating (12) builds the first estimate from the plans and your past jobs, ready for your numbers. Trades (10) sends every job out for pricing and chases the quotes until they're in. Scheduling (11) books the trades in order and tells every one of them when the job moves. Variations (7) writes up every change on site, gets the client's sign-off and adds it to the next claim. Claims (8) sends the progress claim at every stage and chases it when it's late. Site ops (13) keeps diaries, safety forms and deliveries tracked. Underneath sits one knowledge base they all read: past jobs, rate sheets, contract templates and safety procedures. One job per agent. One written workflow per job. Estimates wait for your numbers, the client signs every variation first, and every morning one page tells you what's on site today, what's late and who's waiting on you.
